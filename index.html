My Finances

«Мої фінанси» is an Android app for personal income and expense tracking, created by Ruslan Shostak. It offers categories, currencies, charts, import and export, and optional Google Drive backups. The interface is available in Ukrainian and English.

Core record keeping works on your device without a Google account. The app contains no advertising or usage analytics.
